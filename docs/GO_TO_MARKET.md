# Go-To-Market

## 1. Product in one sentence

The AI Energy Intelligence Terminal turns free, public energy-market data into
operational decisions — when to shift load, charge storage, or hold — with
forecasts, a risk gate, and a data-grounded advisor, across multiple countries.

## 2. Problem

Electricity prices now swing wildly within a single day (negative at noon,
spiking at dusk). The people exposed to this — flexibility operators, prosumers,
energy managers, and analysts — either:

- fly blind on utility portals that show prices but not **decisions**, or
- pay for Bloomberg/enterprise energy desks that are overkill and expensive.

There is a gap between "raw price data" and "what should I do in the next 24
hours." This product fills it.

## 3. Who it's for (ICP)

| Segment | Pain | What they use |
|---|---|---|
| **Flexibility & storage operators** (batteries, aggregators) | Need to time charge/discharge to the price curve | Flexibility optimizer, simulator, forecast |
| **Prosumers / smart-home & EV owners** | Want to charge cheap and avoid peaks | Power prices, screener, advisor |
| **Energy managers** (facilities, SMEs) | Shift discretionary load, prove savings | Screener, flexibility, weekly savings report |
| **Analysts / traders (junior desks)** | Fast market read across zones | Cockpit, forecast, regimes, reports |
| **Portfolio/demo & educational** | Understand market mechanics | Whole terminal on sample data |

**Beachhead:** Denmark (live DK1/DK2 data, extreme volatility, open data) →
flexibility operators and technical prosumers. Expand to Germany next.

## 4. Value proposition

- **Decisions, not just data.** Every module ends in an action: cheapest hours,
  a battery/EV schedule, a SAFE/WARN/CRITICAL gate, a recommendation.
- **Trustworthy by design.** The risk engine blocks recommendations when data is
  stale or confidence is low. The advisor is grounded in platform data (it
  cannot invent numbers).
- **Multi-country from day one.** One schema, four countries, clear "live vs
  sample" labeling.
- **Low cost to run.** Built entirely on free public data and free-tier cloud;
  the default advisor is rule-based (no LLM bill).

## 6. Pricing model (proposed)

| Tier | Price | For | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 | Prosumers, students, evaluators | 1 country, day-ahead prices, screener, advisor (rule-based), daily report |
| **Pro** | €19–49/mo | Technical prosumers, SMEs | All countries, forecasts + regimes, flexibility optimizer, simulator, weekly savings reports, alerts |
| **Operator** | €199+/mo or usage | Storage/flex operators | API access, higher-resolution/scheduled ingestion, custom asset models, priority support |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Desks, utilities | SSO, SLAs, private deployment, LLM advisor, bespoke data adapters |

Rationale: the free tier is genuinely useful (drives adoption and word of
mouth), Pro monetizes the forecasting/optimization that costs real modeling
effort, and Operator/Enterprise monetize API + scale.

## 7. Go-to-market motion

**Phase 1 — Launch (0–3 months): product-led, community-first.**
- Ship the free tier publicly (Vercel + Railway + Neon).
- Content: "Why Danish power went negative today," weekly regime recaps, savings
  case studies generated by the reports module.
- Distribute in energy/flexibility/solar/EV and r/energy-style communities,
  LinkedIn, and a simple landing page. Open-source-friendly posture.

**Phase 2 — Convert (3–9 months): free → Pro.**
- Email/weekly-report loop keeps users returning; gate forecasts + optimizer +
  alerts behind Pro. Add Germany live to widen the market.
- Design-partner 3–5 flexibility operators for the Operator tier; use their
  needs to prioritize the API and custom asset models.

**Phase 3 — Scale (9–18 months): operators & enterprise.**
- Publish the API, add US (ERCOT) and Japan (JEPX) live.
- Outbound to storage operators and energy retailers; pilot enterprise
  deployments with SSO and the LLM advisor.

## 8. Key metrics

- **Acquisition:** signups, weekly active zones viewed, report opens.
- **Activation:** % who connect a zone and view a forecast in week 1.
- **Retention:** weekly-report open rate, WAU/MAU.
- **Revenue:** free→Pro conversion, Operator pipeline, net revenue retention.
- **Product truth:** forecast MAE/RMSE trend (already computed per request),
  risk-gate accuracy, advisor answer usefulness.

## 10. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Data-source licensing (Open-Meteo non-commercial) | Confirm terms before charging; swap to licensed weather for commercial tiers. |
| Forecast credibility | MAE/RMSE shown transparently; risk gate prevents over-claiming; upgrade models in `ml/`. |
| "Just another dashboard" perception | Lead with decisions and savings reports, not charts. |
| Commodity price data | Moat is the decision layer + multi-country + trust, not the raw data. |
| Regulatory/advice liability | Position as decision support; the risk gate and disclaimers make limits explicit. |
